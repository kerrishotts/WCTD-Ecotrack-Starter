Mutation Detective

> section_11 / async

Promise Race

EcoTrack needs three pieces of data from three servers. Each takes a random amount of time. Compare waiting for them one at a time with asking for all of them at once.

One at a time

const city = await findCity("Albany"); const air = await getAirQuality(lat, lon); const temp = await getTemperature(lat, lon);

All at once

const [city, air, temp] = await Promise.all([ findCity("Albany"), getAirQuality(lat, lon), getTemperature(lat, lon), ]);

Watch out: Promise.all only works when the requests don't depend on each other. Here, the air quality request really needs the city's latitude and longitude first. Which steps could truly run in parallel?
