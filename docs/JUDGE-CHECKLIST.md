# Judge-Ready Checklist

Go through this with your whole team before demo day. Each section matches a category on the Hack-A-Thon judging rubric (20 points each, 160 total).

## Website features (rubric: Website features)
- [ ] **Home page** shows what the app does within 5 seconds of landing
- [ ] **About page** explains the problem, the solution, and who built it
- [ ] **Contact page** has a form that checks its input and confirms it was sent
- [ ] **Social media integration**: share buttons work, and your team's accounts are linked (`src/siteConfig.js`)
- [ ] Bonus: an integration beyond social media (EcoTrack has live air quality data)

## It works (rubric: User Experience & Functionality)
- [ ] Every page loads with **no red errors** in the browser console (F12 → Console)
- [ ] Every link and button does something; nothing leads to a dead end
- [ ] Loading and error states: turn off Wi-Fi and try the live data. Is the message friendly?
- [ ] Refresh the page. Is saved data still there?
- [ ] Try it on a **phone-sized screen** (F12 → device toolbar). Can you read and tap everything?
- [ ] Someone **outside your team** can use it without you explaining anything

## Accessible to everyone
- [ ] Every input has a visible `<label>`
- [ ] Every image has `alt` text (or `alt=""` if it's decoration)
- [ ] You can reach and use everything with the **Tab** and **Enter** keys
- [ ] Text is easy to read against its background

## Theme and impact (rubric: Appropriateness to theme, Originality & Impact)
- [ ] You can name the UN Sustainable Development Goal(s) you address, and say it on the About page
- [ ] You can explain in one sentence **who** this helps and **how**
- [ ] You know what makes your idea different from what already exists

## AI as a team member (rubric: How did you treat AI as a team member?)
- [ ] `AI-LOG.md` has entries from **every** team member
- [ ] The log shows AI's different roles: brainstorming, explaining, design reviews, code review, testing
- [ ] At least one entry where AI was **wrong** and how you caught it
- [ ] Everyone on the team can explain any line of code a judge points to

## Safe and responsible
- [ ] No passwords, API keys or secrets anywhere in your code or repo
- [ ] No real personal information (yours or anyone else's) in sample data or screenshots
- [ ] You didn't paste private information into an AI tool

## Technical difficulty (rubric: Technical Difficulty)
- [ ] You can name the hardest technical problem you solved, and how
- [ ] `npm run build` works with no errors

## Collaboration (rubric: Collaboration with US and India teams)
- [ ] You shared your idea with at least one other team and wrote down their feedback
- [ ] You can describe one change you made because of feedback

## Presentation (rubric: Presentation)
- [ ] Your demo follows `docs/DEMO-SCRIPT.md` and fits the time limit
- [ ] Every team member has a speaking part, and the handoffs are rehearsed
- [ ] You've rehearsed the live demo at least twice, including what to do if the internet fails
