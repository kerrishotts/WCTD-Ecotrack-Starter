# Demo Script Template

A demo is a story, not a tour of every button. Aim for **3 minutes**, and give every team member a part. Fill in the brackets, then rehearse with a timer.

| Time | Who | What to say and show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 – 0:30 | [Name] | **The problem.** Who has it, and why it matters. Name your UN Sustainable Development Goal. *"Climate change feels too big for one person. Teens want to help but can't see their impact."* |
| 0:30 – 0:45 | [Name] | **The solution in one sentence.** *"EcoTrack lets you log small sustainable actions and watch them add up, with live air quality for your city."* |
| 0:45 – 2:00 | [Name] | **The live demo.** Show the one path that tells the story: log an action → mark it done → the score updates → check your city's air quality → the About page shows the total impact. Don't click around randomly. |
| 2:00 – 2:30 | [Name] | **How you built it, and how AI helped.** One technical challenge you solved. One time AI helped, and one time you had to correct it (from your AI Log). |
| 2:30 – 3:00 | [Name] | **What's next and thank you.** One feature you'd add next and who it would help. Thank your mentors and the teams who gave you feedback. |

## Before you present

- [ ] Open the app in a fresh browser window, already on the Home page
- [ ] Close other tabs and turn off notifications
- [ ] Zoom the browser to 125% so judges can read it
- [ ] Have a **backup**: screenshots or a short screen recording in case the internet fails
- [ ] Know who answers which kinds of questions (design, code, AI, impact)

## Questions judges often ask

- What was the hardest part, and how did you solve it?
- How did you decide what to build first?
- Show me a place where AI got something wrong.
- Who did what on your team?
- How would you make this reach more people?
